The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names and the fix that is queued. It comes in through the app, email and the booking-v2 form.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour run booking-v3. Their people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom looks the same as parser, a swapped day and month, but the parser fix does not touch it: only reports that came through the v3 form belong here. The form’s hint, or the way it reads the field, needs its own change.

one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a swap. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May (R-41202). A client in Lisbon got Tuesday and one in Toronto got Monday for the same meeting (R-41344). An invite arrived before its booking (R-41236). Every June booking was one day off (R-41209). These read as a time-zone shift. The desk already noted them as one-day moves and filed them under parser by tenant; they stay counted there too. No parser or hint fix moves a date back by one day.

How this was counted

  • One id, one report: the export has rows twice where its pages overlap.
  • The cause comes from the tenant and the way in (causes.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guessed from the reporter’s browser and got many of them wrong.
  • One-day moves are the desk’s own list (desk-notes.json), checked against every body: they are the only reports whose dates differ by one day and not by a day/month swap.
  • Subjects say nothing about the cause. Five subjects turn up under all three problems, which is why merging by subject (decision 0001) put them in one ticket.

What changes for the desk

  • #4471 stays the parser ticket. Close it when the parser fix ships, not before: then check that new booking-v3 form reports and one-day moves still arrive, and file them under their own tickets.
  • Before merging a date report, ask two things the subject cannot tell you: did it come through a booking-v3 form, and did the date swap or move by one day?
